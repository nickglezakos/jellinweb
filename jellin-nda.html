Background & Recitals

WHEREAS, Party A has conceived, developed, and operates Jellin — a loyalty and rewards platform that connects businesses with their customers through the simplest possible interaction: a phone number, an email, or a QR scan. No app download is required, no plastic cards, no lengthy registration — just instant, tangible rewards that make everyday transactions more meaningful.

Jellin gives any business — from a single café to a hotel network to an entire shopping mall — the ability to run professional loyalty programmes, instant promotions, and cross-business reward ecosystems. Customers participate by sharing only a phone number or email. Rewards are delivered via SMS, email, or the optional Jellin app. For customers, loyalty becomes effortless; for businesses, repeat visits become measurable.

The platform supports three distinct reward mechanisms: counting coupons (earned after a set number of check-ins), instant coupons (assigned on the spot by staff), and sweepstake coupons (awarded randomly within a date range). A cross-business group architecture allows Jells earned at one business to carry value at partner businesses, enabling mall ecosystems, hotel networks, and multi-location brands to share a single loyalty experience.

Jellin is GDPR-compliant by design, requires only a phone number or email, and is built to serve independent shops and enterprise operators alike.

WHEREAS, Party B is a business entity with relevant market reach, industry relationships, and commercial expertise capable of identifying, approaching, and converting prospective business clients for the Jellin platform.

WHEREAS, in connection with evaluating and executing a mutual business development collaboration, Party A may disclose to Party B certain confidential, proprietary, and commercially sensitive information, and both Parties wish to define the terms governing such disclosure, their respective obligations, and the commercial arrangement for Party B's business development contributions.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises contained herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows.

Definitions

"Confidential Information" means any and all information disclosed by Party A to Party B, in any form or medium, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. This includes, without limitation: the Jellin platform's source code, architecture, product roadmap, pricing strategies, business plans, financial projections, customer data, marketing strategies, technical documentation, trade secrets, sales methodologies, partner agreements, and any other proprietary data.

"Business Development Activities" means the identification, introduction, solicitation, negotiation support, and conversion of prospective business clients on behalf of Party A for the purpose of subscribing to or contracting for the Jellin platform.

"Referred Client" means any business entity that (a) is first introduced to Party A by Party B, (b) enters into a paid subscription or contract with Party A for the Jellin platform, and (c) was not previously known to or in active discussions with Party A at the time of introduction.

"Project" means each individual Business Development Activity or group of related activities resulting in a confirmed Referred Client or a defined deliverable agreed upon in writing between the Parties.

"Remuneration" means the fee, commission, or other compensation payable by Party A to Party B in respect of a Project, as agreed in a Project Schedule.

"Project Schedule" means a written document executed by both Parties specifying the scope, deliverables, targets, and Remuneration for a particular Project.

"Term" means the period commencing on the Effective Date and continuing for [___] months / years, unless extended or terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

Non-Disclosure Obligations

2.1 Obligation of Confidentiality. Party B agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose, reproduce, summarise, or otherwise communicate any Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of Party A.

2.2 Permitted Use. Party B may use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing Business Development Activities under this Agreement. No other use is permitted.

2.3 Need-to-Know Basis. Party B shall restrict access to Confidential Information to its employees, officers, contractors, and agents who have a genuine need to know such information for the purposes of this Agreement and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than those set out herein.

2.4 Protection Standard. Party B shall protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it applies to its own confidential information of comparable sensitivity, but in no event less than reasonable care.

2.5 Exclusions. The obligations of this clause shall not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of Party B; (b) was rightfully in Party B's possession prior to disclosure by Party A; (c) is independently developed by Party B without reference to the Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by applicable law or court order, provided Party B gives Party A prompt written notice and cooperates with any effort to seek a protective order.

2.6 Mutual Non-Disclosure. To the extent Party B discloses its own proprietary business information to Party A in the course of this Agreement, Party A shall observe equivalent confidentiality obligations with respect to such information.

2.7 Return of Information. Upon request or upon termination of this Agreement, Party B shall promptly return or, at Party A's election, certifiably destroy all materials containing Confidential Information and shall not retain any copies thereof.

2.8 Duration of Confidentiality. The obligations of this clause shall survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement and shall remain in effect for a period of [three (3)] years thereafter.

3

Business Development Engagement

3.1 Appointment. Party A hereby appoints Party B as a non-exclusive business development partner for the purpose of identifying and introducing prospective clients for the Jellin platform during the Term. This appointment does not grant Party B the right to enter into binding agreements on behalf of Party A.

3.2 Scope of Activities. Party B shall conduct Business Development Activities including but not limited to: identifying target businesses suited to the Jellin platform; initiating and managing introductory communications; presenting the Jellin value proposition to prospects; supporting qualification and initial negotiation; and facilitating the handover of prospects to Party A for contract execution.

3.3 Project-by-Project Basis. Each Project shall be defined and agreed upon in a separate Project Schedule signed by both Parties before commencement. No Project Schedule shall be binding until executed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

3.4 Reporting. Party B shall provide Party A with regular written progress updates — no less than [monthly] — detailing prospects approached, status of discussions, and any material developments per active Project.

3.5 Standards of Conduct. Party B shall conduct Business Development Activities in a professional manner, in compliance with all applicable laws, and in a way that upholds and enhances the reputation of the Jellin brand. Party B shall not make any representations about Jellin that are inaccurate, misleading, or inconsistent with materials approved by Party A.

3.6 Approval of Materials. Party B shall use only marketing and sales materials approved in advance by Party A. Party B shall seek written approval before distributing any new materials that reference the Jellin platform, brand, or Confidential Information.

3.7 No Solicitation of Existing Clients. Party B shall not approach or solicit any existing clients of Party A without prior written consent from Party A.

Remuneration

4.1 Per-Project Compensation. Party B shall be entitled to Remuneration for each Project successfully completed, as specified in the relevant Project Schedule. The Parties acknowledge and agree that Remuneration shall be determined on a per-project basis, reflecting the nature, scope, and commercial value of each Project.

4.2 Forms of Remuneration. Remuneration may take one or more of the following forms, as agreed in each Project Schedule:

  • A fixed fee upon achievement of defined milestones or deliverables;
  • A percentage-based commission on the first-year contract value of each Referred Client; or
  • A combination of fixed fee and recurring commission.

4.3 Payment Terms. Unless otherwise specified in a Project Schedule, Remuneration shall be invoiced by Party B upon the agreed trigger event (e.g., contract signing by a Referred Client) and shall be payable by Party A within [thirty (30)] calendar days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.4 Conditions for Remuneration. Remuneration shall be due only where: (a) the Referred Client was introduced exclusively by Party B; (b) the introduction is documented in writing prior to or at the time of first contact; (c) a binding paid agreement between Party A and the Referred Client has been fully executed; and (d) no breach of this Agreement by Party B has occurred in connection with the relevant Project.

4.5 Expenses. Unless expressly included in a Project Schedule, Party B shall be responsible for all of its own costs and expenses incurred in carrying out Business Development Activities. Party A shall not reimburse expenses without prior written approval.

4.6 Taxes. Each Party is responsible for its own tax obligations arising from this Agreement. Party B shall issue invoices in compliance with applicable tax regulations. No withholding shall apply unless required by law.

4.7 No Exclusivity or Guaranteed Volume. Nothing in this Agreement guarantees Party B a minimum volume of Projects, referrals, or Remuneration. Party A retains the right to engage other business development partners and to pursue its own direct sales activities.

5.3 Brand Guidelines. Party B shall use the Jellin brand strictly in accordance with any brand guidelines provided by Party A and shall not alter, modify, or create derivative works from any Jellin brand assets without prior written consent.

5.4 Work Product. Any materials, reports, analyses, or other deliverables created by Party B specifically for the purposes of this Agreement and referencing the Jellin platform shall, upon creation, be the property of Party A unless otherwise agreed in writing in a Project Schedule.

Non-Solicitation & Non-Competition

6.1 Non-Solicitation of Staff. During the Term and for a period of [twelve (12)] months following its expiry or termination, each Party agrees not to directly solicit, recruit, or attempt to employ any employee, officer, or contractor of the other Party who was involved in the performance of this Agreement.

6.2 Non-Competition. During the Term, Party B shall not, without Party A's prior written consent, develop, promote, distribute, or provide services in relation to any product or service that directly competes with the Jellin platform in the agreed territory.

6.3 Conflict of Interest. Party B shall promptly notify Party A in writing of any relationship, engagement, or circumstance that creates or may create a conflict of interest with Party B's obligations under this Agreement.

Term & Termination

8.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for the Term unless terminated earlier in accordance with this clause.

8.2 Termination for Convenience. Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing [thirty (30)] calendar days' written notice to the other Party. Any Projects in progress at the time of notice shall be completed or wound down in good faith unless otherwise agreed.

8.3 Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other Party: (a) commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to remedy it within [fifteen (15)] calendar days of written notice of such breach; (b) becomes insolvent, enters liquidation, or is subject to analogous proceedings; or (c) engages in fraud, wilful misconduct, or conduct materially damaging to the other Party's reputation.

8.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination: (a) all licences granted under this Agreement shall immediately cease; (b) each Party shall comply with Clause 2.7 regarding the return or destruction of Confidential Information; (c) Party B shall be entitled to Remuneration only in respect of Projects for which the trigger event occurred before the effective date of termination; and (d) clauses that by their nature survive termination (including Clauses 2, 5, 6.1, 9, and 10) shall remain in full force.
