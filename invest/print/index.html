01

What is Jellin

Jellin is loyalty software that turns a phone number or email into a working rewards program — no app download required. Businesses run counting, instant, and sweepstake coupons; customers redeem by SMS, email, QR, or the optional app. One platform serves a single café and a multi-partner hotel or mall network.

Who buys — and why now

Real buyers, real jobs: people today hit a friction wall with stamp cards and heavy loyalty apps — with Jellin they enroll in seconds and see value on the same visit.

An independent café owner can now replace a lost punch card with “Your number?” — free coffee on the 5th visit, measured repeats.

A gym chain ops lead can now let Jells accumulate across every branch — one identity, employee-level visibility.

A hotel manager can now Jell a guest at check-in and push partner café or boutique offers by SMS — without a custom build.

A mall or sports operator can now connect purchases to experience rewards — jacket → ticket, store → mall gift threshold.

Platform reasons that cut across every persona

Zero barrier Not app-first

Phone, email, or QR. Same reward path with or without the app.

Immediate rewards Not months of wait

Counting, instant, and sweepstake coupons — payoff on a short cycle.

Cross-business Not siloed cards

Groups and partner operators — earn here, redeem there.

Privacy-light Not data harvest

Phone/email only for loyalty customers. GDPR-oriented by design.

Every channel Not one inbox

SMS, email QR, in-app — meet the customer where they already are.

Ops + analytics Not vanity metrics

Owner / Manager / Employee roles; redemptions by program, site, staff.

03

What changes

Customers are not locked to an app wall. Staff ask one question; the platform tracks visits, issues coupons, and delivers rewards on the channel the customer already uses — including when they never install anything.

Friction Collapsed

Enrollment is phone or email. QR for app users. Same ledger either way.

Action Not passive points

Counting thresholds, instant staff offers, and dated sweepstakes that fire without a project.

Network Not one store

Internal and partner groups — invite operators; redeem across the host program.

Incumbents sell

  • App downloads & plastic cards
  • Slow time-to-reward
  • Siloed brand wallets
  • Heavy CRM / data collection

Jellin ships

  • Number / email / QR first
  • Rewards on a short cycle
  • Partner groups on one identity
  • Minimal personal data by design
