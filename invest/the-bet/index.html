The bet

Jellin’s differentiators are how you enter the loyalty TAM where incumbents can only sell another app or another plastic card — then expand café-to-network without a download ceiling.

Prove paid adoption in Greece at median 5% of core SAM; then deploy the same product into the U.S. venue base at 1.5%.
