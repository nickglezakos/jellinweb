Part I

Introduction

Jellin is a loyalty and rewards platform that connects businesses with their customers through the simplest possible interaction: a phone number, an email, or a QR scan. No app download required, no plastic cards, no lengthy registration — just instant, tangible rewards that make everyday transactions more meaningful.

What Jellin Does

Jellin gives any business — from a single café to a hotel network to an entire shopping mall — the ability to run professional loyalty programs, instant promotions, and cross-business reward ecosystems. Customers participate by sharing only a phone number or email. Rewards are delivered via SMS, email, or the optional Jellin app.

For customers, loyalty becomes effortless. For businesses, repeat visits become measurable.

The Problem Jellin Solves

Most loyalty programs fail because they demand too much from customers and deliver too little in return. They require app downloads, lengthy forms, plastic cards, or passwords. Customers lose interest before ever reaching a reward. Businesses invest in programs they can't measure.

On the business side, independent shops lack the tools that large chains take for granted. Multi-location operators struggle with siloed customer data. Hotels and malls have no practical way to connect partner businesses into a shared loyalty experience.

Jellin eliminates these barriers on both sides.

Five Strong Points

1. Zero-Barrier Participation

A phone number or email is all it takes. There is no app to download, no account to create, no card to carry. A customer can be enrolled in seconds — at a café counter, a hotel reception desk, or a mall checkout.

This matters because the biggest obstacle to loyalty program success is sign-up friction. Every additional step loses customers. Jellin reduces the enrollment to a single question: "Your number?"

Technical foundation: Jellin supports two check-in methods — QR scan (for app users) and phone number / email lookup (for everyone else). Both methods lead to the same reward experience.

2. Rewards That Feel Immediate

Jellin doesn't make people wait months to see value. A free coffee on the 5th visit. A match ticket with a jacket purchase. A discount the moment you check into a hotel. The payoff is fast and tangible.

The platform supports three distinct coupon types:

  • Counting coupons — earned after a set number of check-ins (e.g., "free coffee every 5 visits")
  • Instant coupons — assigned on the spot by staff. Perfect for welcome offers and partner promotions.
  • Sweepstake coupons — awarded randomly to enrolled customers within a date range.

3. One Identity, Many Places

Jells earned at one business carry over to partner businesses. A single phone number connects a customer to an entire neighborhood, mall, or brand network — without juggling separate loyalty cards or apps.

This cross-business capability works through a group-based architecture:

  • Internal groups — for own locations, where employees can assign both Jells and instant coupons.
  • Partner groups — for satellite businesses, where invited external operators can assign instant coupons under the host's loyalty program.

4. Privacy Without Compromise

Customers share only a phone number or email. There is no personal data harvesting, no invasive tracking, no behavioral profiling beyond what the customer explicitly opts into. Jellin is GDPR-compliant by design.

This is a competitive advantage, not just a compliance checkbox. In a market where consumers are increasingly wary of data collection, Jellin's minimal-data approach builds trust.

5. Works on Every Channel

Whether someone prefers SMS, email, a QR scan, or the full app experience, Jellin meets them where they are:

  • Email — with an embedded QR code that can be shown at any point of sale
  • SMS — with a QR link for instant access
  • App — for customers who prefer the in-app experience

Why Jellin Is Different

Traditional loyalty platforms force a choice: simple but limited, or powerful but complex. Jellin offers both simplicity and depth:

  • A café owner can set up a "free coffee every 5 visits" campaign in minutes — and that's all they need.
  • A hotel chain can build a cross-business ecosystem with partner cafés, boutiques, and tour operators — all managed from one dashboard.
  • Both use the same platform. The complexity is there when needed, invisible when it's not.

The combination of zero-friction participation, cross-business collaboration, and multi-channel delivery means Jellin works for the widest possible range of businesses and customers — without requiring either side to change how they already operate.

Part III

Technical Modules

Core technical modules of the Jellin platform — how loyalty programs, coupons, check-ins, user roles, groups, cross-business collaboration, delivery, and analytics work together.

1. Loyalty Programs

A business owns a single Jellin account and can create multiple loyalty programs under it. Each loyalty program defines the rules for how customers earn and redeem rewards. Loyalty programs are assigned to groups, which determine which users and points of sale can operate under them.

2. Coupons

Each loyalty program supports three coupon types:

2.1 Counting Coupon

Earned after X consecutive check-ins. The business defines the threshold (e.g., "free coffee after 5 check-ins").

How it works: Customer checks in → Employee assigns Jells → At threshold, coupon is automatically generated and awarded.

2.2 Instant Coupon

Assigned immediately by the employee — no accumulation required.

Use cases: Welcome offers (hotel check-in), cross-business partner promotions, one-time incentives, event-based rewards.

2.3 Sweepstake Coupon

Awarded randomly to enrolled customers within a configured date range — no employee action or check-in required.

Configuration: Loyalty program (base), date range (window), coupon quantity (how many distributed randomly).

Use cases: Slow-period engagement, surprise-and-delight, re-engaging dormant customers, lottery-style promotions.

3. Check-In Methods

App Check-In (QR Scan)

  1. Customer opens Jellin app, presents profile QR code
  2. Employee scans with Jellin Business App
  3. Check-in recorded instantly

No-App Check-In (Phone / Email)

  1. Customer tells employee their mobile number or email
  2. Employee enters it in the Jellin Business App
  3. Check-in recorded

After check-in (both methods), the employee can assign Jells toward a counting coupon or assign an instant coupon directly.

4. User Roles & Permissions

RoleCapabilitiesAnalytics

5. Groups

Groups are the organizational unit connecting loyalty programs, users, and points of sale. A business can create as many groups as needed.

Group TypePurposeExample
Own networkOrganize internal points of saleA gym chain creates a group per city
Satellite / partnerEnable cross-business collaborationA hotel creates a group for partner businesses
Campaign-specificIsolate specific promotionsA mall creates a seasonal holiday program group

6. Cross-Business Collaboration

Jellin enables cross-business rewards through groups, loyalty programs, and external user invitations:

  1. Business A (e.g., a hotel) creates a partner group
  2. Business A activates loyalty programs within that group
  3. Business A invites users from Business B (e.g., a café) via email
  4. The invited user joins as an employee/operator within that group
  5. The invited user can assign instant coupons to customers under Business A's program
Typical setup: A business maintains Internal groups (own shops — full Jells + instant coupons) and Partner groups (satellite businesses — instant coupons only). A hotel guest earns a coupon at check-in and redeems it at a partner café — without the café needing its own Jellin account.
