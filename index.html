Hospitality Ecosystem

The Hotel Guest Scenario

A traveler checks into a hotel. The concierge "Jells" their number. Instantly, the guest receives a welcome SMS with a QR link.

They can now get loyalty points at the partnered Cafe around the corner and a discount at the local Apparel Shop, all under the hotel's custom loyalty umbrella.

Campaign: "Neighborhood Explorer Network"

Concierge: Welcome! Let me add you to our local partner rewards. Your number?
Guest: 555-890-...
Concierge: Perfect! You just received 5 instant Jells! That gives you 20% off breakfast and a free coffee at our restaurant!
Guest: Wow, that's great! Can I use it at other places too?
Concierge: Yes! You can also earn more Jells at our partner cafe and boutique down the street.
Guest: [Next morning at restaurant] I'd like to use my breakfast discount.
Server: Of course! Your number? ... Applied! 20% off and your free coffee is on the way!

FAQ

Questions marketers ask before they launch

Everything you need to track loyalty, grow customer acquisition, and prove campaign performance with Jellin.

How do I track and reward loyal customers while finding new ones?

Jellin is an all-in-one loyalty and acquisition platform where you can create and manage loyalty programs, track customer behavior, and reward your most loyal customers. It helps both small businesses and large enterprises grow repeat engagement while attracting new customers.

How can I multiply customers by collaborating with local businesses?

Jellin’s cross-promotion feature lets you partner with local businesses for joint promotions and shared rewards. This expands your reach, strengthens the local business community, and creates a reward network that benefits every partner.

How can I track employee performance and reward great work?

Jellin tracks team transactions and progress, helps you create incentives for strong performance, and gives managers oversight with approval rights whenever needed. It also highlights unusual or out-of-policy transaction patterns early, so you can coach proactively, protect standards, and keep your team motivated.

How do I create a seamless customer onboarding flow?

With phone or email-only onboarding, customers can join your loyalty program without long forms or app downloads. SMS and email work everywhere, reducing friction and increasing sign-up rates.

How can I measure loyalty campaign success?

Jellin campaign analytics provide real-time tracking for redemptions, engagement, and other key metrics. You can use this data to prove results to clients and optimize future campaigns with confidence.

I offer social marketing services. How does Jellin add value for my clients?

Jellin gives your clients one platform for loyalty, acquisition, and customer engagement. You can help them run smarter programs, track behavior, reward loyalty, and unlock local cross-promotions that drive measurable growth.
