2. The service

Jellin lets a business run loyalty programs and related promotions. Depending on the modules enabled for the business account, the service includes:

  • Business accounts. A business registers a workspace (a tenant) with a business name, country, phone number, email address, and password. A VAT number may be provided.   • Loyalty programs and groups. A business can create programs that award stamps (also called Jells) after visits, set how many stamps are required, and set how long a reward stays valid. Programs can be attached to groups, such as a location, a campaign, or a partner network.
  • Visits and rewards. An authorised operator records a visit for a customer identified by a phone number or an existing customer record. The operator can add stamps, redeem a reward, or assign an immediate coupon. A visit can be cancelled by an operator who has permission to do so.
  • Customer contact. Customers can take part with a phone number or email address. An app download is optional. Rewards may be shown in the app or sent by email or SMS with a code the customer can present.
  • Promotional draws. A business can award a set number of coupons at random to customers already enrolled in a program, including filters such as group, program, or last visit. This is a promotional reward run by the business. It is not a cash lottery operated by Jellin.
  • Messages. A business can store message templates and send operational or promotional messages to people enrolled in its programs, and can receive inbox notices inside Jellin. Push notifications may be delivered to a signed-in device.
  • Summaries. Owners and operators with the relevant permission can view summaries of programs and visits.
  • Referral accounts. A separate account type for people who introduce businesses to Jellin. A referral account uses a name, phone number, email address, password, and referral code. It is not a customer loyalty account.
