# Jellin — Introduction

Jellin is a loyalty and rewards platform that connects businesses with their customers through the simplest possible interaction: a phone number, an email, or a QR scan. No app download required, no plastic cards, no lengthy registration — just instant, tangible rewards that make everyday transactions more meaningful.

---

## What Jellin Does

Jellin gives any business — from a single café to a hotel network to an entire shopping mall — the ability to run professional loyalty programs, instant promotions, and cross-business reward ecosystems. Customers participate by sharing only a phone number or email. Rewards are delivered via SMS, email, or the optional Jellin app.

For customers, loyalty becomes effortless. For businesses, repeat visits become measurable.

---

## The Problem Jellin Solves

Most loyalty programs fail because they demand too much from customers and deliver too little in return. They require app downloads, lengthy forms, plastic cards, or passwords. Customers lose interest before ever reaching a reward. Businesses invest in programs they can't measure.

On the business side, independent shops lack the tools that large chains take for granted. Multi-location operators struggle with siloed customer data. Hotels and malls have no practical way to connect partner businesses into a shared loyalty experience.

Jellin eliminates these barriers on both sides.

---

## Five Strong Points

### 1. Zero-Barrier Participation

A phone number or email is all it takes. There is no app to download, no account to create, no card to carry. A customer can be enrolled in seconds — at a café counter, a hotel reception desk, or a mall checkout.

This matters because the biggest obstacle to loyalty program success is sign-up friction. Every additional step loses customers. Jellin reduces the enrollment to a single question: *"Your number?"*

**Technical foundation:** Jellin supports two check-in methods — QR scan (for app users) and phone number / email lookup (for everyone else). Both methods lead to the same reward experience, ensuring no customer is excluded.

### 2. Rewards That Feel Immediate

Jellin doesn't make people wait months to see value. A free coffee on the 5th visit. A match ticket with a jacket purchase. A discount the moment you check into a hotel. The payoff is fast and tangible.

The platform supports three distinct coupon types that cover every reward scenario:

- **Counting coupons** — earned after a set number of check-ins (e.g., "free coffee every 5 visits"). Customers accumulate Jells with each visit, and the reward triggers automatically at the threshold.
- **Instant coupons** — assigned on the spot by staff. Perfect for welcome offers, partner promotions, and one-time incentives. No accumulation required.
- **Sweepstake coupons** — awarded randomly to enrolled customers within a date range. Creates surprise-and-delight moments and re-engages dormant customers.

This combination means businesses can reward habitual behavior, trigger immediate action, and create excitement — all within one platform.

### 3. One Identity, Many Places

Jells earned at one business carry over to partner businesses. A single phone number connects a customer to an entire neighborhood, mall, or brand network — without juggling separate loyalty cards or apps.

This cross-business capability is Jellin's most distinctive feature. It works through a group-based architecture:

- A business creates **internal groups** for its own locations, where employees can assign both Jells and instant coupons.
- It creates **partner groups** for satellite businesses, where invited external operators can assign instant coupons under the host's loyalty program.
- Customers move between businesses seamlessly — a hotel guest earns Jells at check-in and redeems a coupon at the partner café down the street, all from one phone number.

For multi-location operators, this means unified loyalty across every branch. For ecosystem builders — hotels, malls, business districts — it means shared foot traffic and measurable cross-business value.

### 4. Privacy Without Compromise

Customers share only a phone number or email. There is no personal data harvesting, no invasive tracking, no behavioral profiling beyond what the customer explicitly opts into. Jellin is GDPR-compliant by design.

This is a competitive advantage, not just a compliance checkbox. In a market where consumers are increasingly wary of data collection, Jellin's minimal-data approach builds trust. Customers participate because they feel safe, not surveilled.

### 5. Works on Every Channel

Whether someone prefers SMS, email, a QR scan, or the full app experience, Jellin meets them where they are. Coupons are delivered through:

- **Email** — with an embedded QR code that can be shown at any point of sale
- **SMS** — with a QR link for instant access
- **App** — for customers who prefer the in-app experience

This multi-channel delivery is essential for cross-business scenarios. A coupon earned at a hotel can be emailed to the guest, who then shows the QR code at a partner café across town. No app needed on either side — just an email and a scanner.

## How It Works — In 30 Seconds

**For the customer:**
1. Visit a participating business
2. Share your phone number or scan a QR code
3. Earn Jells or receive an instant coupon
4. Redeem rewards at the same business or any partner in the network

**For the business:**
1. Create a Jellin account and set up loyalty programs
2. Define coupon rules (counting thresholds, instant offers, sweepstakes)
3. Organize locations and partners into groups
4. Staff check in customers and assign rewards
5. Monitor everything through real-time analytics

## Why Jellin Is Different

Traditional loyalty platforms force a choice: simple but limited, or powerful but complex. Jellin offers both simplicity and depth:

- A café owner can set up a "free coffee every 5 visits" campaign in minutes — and that's all they need.
- A hotel chain can build a cross-business ecosystem with partner cafés, boutiques, and tour operators — all managed from one dashboard.
- Both use the same platform. The complexity is there when needed, invisible when it's not.

The combination of **zero-friction participation**, **cross-business collaboration**, and **multi-channel delivery** means Jellin works for the widest possible range of businesses and customers — without requiring either side to change how they already operate.
