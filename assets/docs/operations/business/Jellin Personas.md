# Jellin App — User Personas

This document describes the key personas that interact with the Jellin platform, derived from the real-world scenarios featured on the website and from additional platform messaging.

---

## How Jellin Benefits People's Everyday Life

### Introduction

Most loyalty programs fail because they ask too much and deliver too little. They require app downloads, lengthy registration forms, plastic cards, or remembering yet another password. Jellin removes all of that.

At its core, Jellin improves everyday life through five strong points:

1. **Zero-barrier participation** — A phone number or email is all it takes. No app download, no account creation, no plastic cards to carry. Anyone can participate in seconds, whether they're tech-savvy or not.

2. **Rewards that feel immediate** — Jellin doesn't make people wait months to see value. A free coffee on the 5th visit, a match ticket with a jacket purchase, a discount the moment you check into a hotel — the payoff is fast and tangible.

3. **One identity, many places** — Jells earned at one business carry over to partner businesses. A single phone number connects a customer to an entire neighborhood, mall, or brand network without juggling separate loyalty cards or apps.

4. **Privacy without compromise** — Customers share only a phone number or email. No personal data harvesting, no invasive tracking. GDPR-compliant by design, so people can participate without worrying about their information.

5. **Works on every channel** — Whether someone prefers SMS, email, a QR scan, or the full app experience, Jellin meets them where they are. No one is excluded.

### How Each Persona Benefits

#### The Daily Regular — "Sarah"

**Before Jellin:** Sarah visits her coffee shop every morning but has nothing to show for her loyalty. Maybe there's a paper punch card she keeps losing, or a clunky app she never downloaded. Her regular patronage goes unrecognized.

**With Jellin:** Sarah gives her phone number once. That's it. Every visit is automatically tracked. On her 5th visit she gets her coffee free. She never has to remember a card, open an app, or ask for a stamp. The reward just happens — and so does the feeling of being valued as a regular. Her daily routine now comes with a built-in payoff.

**Everyday impact:** Loyalty becomes effortless. Her morning habit is rewarded without changing anything about how she already lives.

---

#### The Fitness Enthusiast

**Before Jellin:** A gym member who trains at multiple locations has no way to consolidate progress. Each branch feels like starting over. Loyalty to the brand isn't captured anywhere.

**With Jellin:** Every check-in at any location earns Jells. A quick QR scan at the front desk and the points stack up — 2 Jells today, 8 total across three gyms. At 10 Jells, a free personal training session unlocks. Progress is visible, cross-location, and motivating.

**Everyday impact:** Working out at different locations is no longer a fragmented experience. Consistency across the network is rewarded, making it easier to stick to fitness goals — and feel good about it.

---

#### The Hotel Traveler

**Before Jellin:** A traveler checks into a hotel in an unfamiliar city. They don't know where to eat, what to explore, or which local shops are worth visiting. The hotel might hand them a paper flyer. Most of those end up in the bin.

**With Jellin:** The concierge Jells their phone number at check-in. Instantly, the guest gets a welcome SMS with 5 Jells: 20% off breakfast, a free coffee at the hotel restaurant, and partner discounts at the cafe and boutique down the street. No app to download, no map to unfold. The next morning they use the breakfast discount at the restaurant. Later, they discover the partner cafe and earn more Jells there.

**Everyday impact:** Travel feels more connected and rewarding. The guest explores the neighborhood naturally, saves money, and discovers genuinely good local spots — all because one phone number unlocked an entire ecosystem of value.

---

#### The Sports Fan

**Before Jellin:** A fan buys a team jacket online. The purchase and the match-day experience exist in separate worlds. There's no reward for being a loyal supporter beyond the jersey itself.

**With Jellin:** The fan enters their email at checkout. Instantly, they receive a free ticket for Sunday's match — delivered as a QR code via email. At the stadium, they show the QR on their phone and walk right in. Buy gear, get experiences. The connection between supporting the team and living the team is now seamless.

**Everyday impact:** Being a sports fan becomes more rewarding in a tangible way. Merchandise purchases are no longer just transactions — they're keys to experiences. The fan feels recognized and valued by their team.

---

#### The Mall Explorer

**Before Jellin:** A casual shopper visits a mall. Each store has its own loyalty program (or none at all). There's no reason to explore beyond the usual two or three shops. Spending is scattered and unrewarded.

**With Jellin:** At the first checkout, the cashier asks "Your number?" — and with that, the shopper is enrolled in the mall-wide rewards program. Jells earned at the clothing store also count at the bookstore, the food court, and the electronics shop. At 20 Jells, they get a $20 mall gift card. Suddenly, trying a new store isn't just browsing — it's earning.

**Everyday impact:** Shopping becomes an interconnected experience. Exploring new stores feels rewarding rather than aimless. The shopper visits more places, discovers more, and gets tangible value from the time they spend at the mall.

---

#### The Independent Shop Owner

**Before Jellin:** A cafe owner knows regulars by face but has no system to reward them. Marketing means an Instagram post and hoping for the best. There's no data on what drives repeat business.

**With Jellin:** In minutes, the owner sets up a "free coffee every 5 visits" campaign. Customers join with just a phone number — no app push, no awkward form. The dashboard shows who's coming back, how often, and which campaigns drive the most visits. Real-time analytics replace guesswork.

**Everyday impact:** Running a small business becomes less stressful. The owner has a clear, affordable tool to build repeat customers, prove what's working, and compete with bigger brands — without needing a marketing team.

---

#### The Multi-Location Operator

**Before Jellin:** A gym chain or restaurant group manages each location's loyalty separately. Customer data is siloed. There's no visibility into cross-location behavior. Employee performance varies and is hard to track.

**With Jellin:** One unified dashboard covers all locations. Customers earn Jells wherever they go in the network. The operator sees segmented metrics by salepoint, monitors employee performance, sets approval rights, and identifies out-of-policy patterns early. Scaling from 3 locations to 30 doesn't mean rebuilding the system.

**Everyday impact:** Operations become coherent across the network. The operator spends less time putting out fires and more time making strategic decisions backed by real data. Growth stops feeling chaotic.

---

#### The Ecosystem Builder

**Before Jellin:** A hotel manager or mall operator tries to create partnership value between tenant businesses. The coordination is manual — maybe a shared flyer or a verbal recommendation. There's no shared system, no tracking, and no incentive structure that crosses business boundaries.

**With Jellin:** The ecosystem builder creates a white-label loyalty umbrella. A hotel guest earns Jells that work at the partner cafe and the local boutique. A mall shopper earns across every participating store. Partners get shared foot traffic without complex technical setup. The builder manages it all from one dashboard while each partner maintains their own brand presence.

**Everyday impact:** An entire business neighborhood or property comes alive as a connected experience. Partners benefit from each other's customers. The ecosystem builder creates measurable value that keeps tenants happy and attracts new ones.

---

#### The Marketing Professional / Agency

**Before Jellin:** A marketing agency runs social campaigns and email blasts for clients but struggles to prove direct impact on foot traffic, repeat visits, and in-store conversions. Loyalty and acquisition live in separate tools.

**With Jellin:** One platform handles loyalty programs, instant coupons, point systems, bonuses, and member tiers — all delivered via the customer's preferred channel (app, QR, SMS, email). Real-time analytics show exactly how many people redeemed, how often they return, and which campaigns perform best. The agency walks into client meetings with hard numbers, not just impressions.

**Everyday impact:** Client relationships improve because results are provable. Campaign strategy shifts from "hope it works" to data-driven iteration. The agency becomes indispensable — not just a vendor, but a growth partner.

---

#### The Sports / Entertainment Organization

**Before Jellin:** A sports team sells merchandise through one channel and game tickets through another. There's no connection between the two. Fans who spend the most on gear get no special access to experiences.

**With Jellin:** Merchandise purchases automatically trigger experience rewards. Buy a jacket, get a free match ticket. Buy season gear, unlock VIP access. It all flows through email + QR — no app needed for the fan, no complex integration for the organization. Online purchases and stadium entry are connected through one system.

**Everyday impact:** Fan engagement deepens because every purchase reinforces the emotional connection to the team. Revenue streams (merchandise and tickets) reinforce each other instead of competing. The organization builds a loyalty loop that turns casual buyers into devoted fans.

---

#### The Cashier / Receptionist

**Before Jellin:** Frontline staff at businesses with loyalty programs often deal with clunky POS integrations, forgotten plastic cards, or customer frustration. Enrolling a new customer takes too long and holds up the line.

**With Jellin:** The workflow is simple: "Your number?" — type it in or scan a QR code. The system instantly shows the customer's status, how many Jells they have, and what reward is next. New customer enrollment takes seconds. Staff can confidently communicate rewards without needing special training.

**Everyday impact:** Work becomes smoother and less stressful. Staff feel empowered to create positive customer interactions rather than being held back by cumbersome systems. Faster transactions mean shorter lines and happier customers — which makes the workday better for everyone.

## Key Themes Across All Personas

- **Minimal friction**: Phone number or email is the only data required — no app downloads, no lengthy forms.
- **Multi-channel flexibility**: App, QR, SMS, and email delivery ensures every customer can participate regardless of tech comfort level.
- **Cross-business value**: Jells earned at one business can be redeemed at another, creating network effects that benefit all partners.
- **Scalability**: The same platform works for a single cafe and a multi-brand mall ecosystem.
- **Privacy first**: Only phone numbers or emails are stored. GDPR compliant. Trust is built into the design.
- **Instant gratification**: Rewards are immediate and tangible — free coffee, discounts, match tickets, gift cards.
