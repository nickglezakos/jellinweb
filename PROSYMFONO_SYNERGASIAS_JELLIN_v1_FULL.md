## 2. Description of the Jellin Platform

### 2.1 General Description

**Jellin** is a loyalty and rewards platform that enables businesses to operate frictionless customer engagement programs through phone number, email, or QR-based participation. It combines loyalty programs, instant promotions, cross-business reward ecosystems, and analytics into a unified platform for single-location businesses, multi-location operators, and partner networks.

### 2.2 Product Portfolio

The platform offers a unified loyalty and rewards system built around the following core functional modules:

| Module | Description | Status |
| -------- | ----------- | -------- |
| **Loyalty Programs** | Businesses create and manage multiple loyalty programs under one account, each defining how customers earn and redeem rewards. | Ready |
| **Counting Coupons** | Rewards triggered automatically after a customer accumulates a defined number of Jells through repeat check-ins. | Ready |
| **Instant Coupons** | Offers assigned immediately by staff at the point of interaction, without any accumulation requirement. | Ready |
| **Sweepstake Coupons** | Randomly awarded coupons distributed to enrolled customers during a configured date range. | Ready |
| **Cross-Business Groups** | Group-based collaboration that allows one business to invite partner businesses into the same reward ecosystem. | Ready |
| **Multi-Channel Delivery & Analytics** | Coupon delivery via email, SMS, and app, combined with analytics by program, group, employee, customer, and location. | Ready |

### 2.3 Technological Advantages

- **Zero-Barrier Participation:** Customers can participate with only a phone number, email, or QR scan, without requiring app installation
- **Immediate and Measurable Rewards:** Businesses can combine repeat-visit incentives, one-time offers, and randomized campaign rewards in one platform
- **Cross-Business Ecosystems:** A single loyalty identity can work across multiple businesses, locations, and partner networks
- **Privacy-First Approach:** Minimal customer data collection with GDPR-conscious participation flows
- **Multi-Channel Delivery:** Rewards can be delivered via email, SMS, or the optional Jellin app
- **Operational Analytics:** Owners and managers can monitor performance across programs, groups, employees, locations, coupon types, and customer activity

### 2.4 Deployment and Operating Model

The platform supports a range of practical business environments and operating models:

| Mode | Use Case |
| ---- | -------- |
| **Single Location** | Independent cafes, boutiques, salons, restaurants, and local shops |
| **Multi-Location Network** | Gym chains, restaurant groups, hospitality operators, and franchises |
| **Partner Ecosystem** | Hotels, malls, business districts, and organizations connecting third-party merchants |
| **No-App Participation** | Walk-in customers joining via phone number or email at the point of sale |
| **App-Enabled Experience** | Customers who prefer to use the Jellin mobile app and QR-based check-in |

### 2.5 Target Market and Size

Jellin addresses the broader loyalty, local commerce engagement, hospitality promotion, and partner-network rewards market.

| Sector | Applications |
| ------ | ------------ |
| **Hospitality** | Hotels, resorts, and concierge-led partner reward ecosystems |
| **Food & Beverage** | Cafes, restaurants, bakeries, bars, and recurring-visit promotions |
| **Retail** | Boutiques, local stores, malls, and cross-store loyalty initiatives |
| **Fitness & Wellness** | Gyms, studios, beauty and wellness chains with multi-location rewards |
| **Sports / Entertainment** | Ticket-linked promotions, merchandise rewards, and fan engagement campaigns |

### 5.3 Exceptions

- Was publicly known at the time of disclosure without fault of the receiving party

- Was lawfully obtained from a third party without a confidentiality obligation

### 5.4 Duration of Confidentiality

The confidentiality obligations under this section remain in force throughout the duration of the cooperation and for **three (3) years** after termination, expiry, or cancellation of this document, regardless of cause.
